# Pin Travel Tracker

A simple world map for marking places you have visited or lived in. It works offline, and all your data stays on your own device.

**Live app: [https://1qj.github.io/pin-travel-tracker/](https://1qj.github.io/pin-travel-tracker/)**

## Getting started

Open the app: [https://1qj.github.io/pin-travel-tracker/](https://1qj.github.io/pin-travel-tracker/)

After the first visit, the app should also work without an internet connection.

Install it on your device:

- **Safari (macOS Sonoma or newer):** open the address, then choose File > Add to Dock.
- **Chrome:** open the address, then use the three-dot menu > Cast, save and share > Install page as app.

## How to use it

- Search for a city in the search field, or tap the map to drop a pin.
- Every pin is one of three kinds: Visited, Lived there or Want to go.
- Under "Colors", choose the pin color for each kind: red, blue, gold, green or purple. By default, Visited is green, Lived there is gold and Want to go is red.
- Zoom in close to see city names. Cities you have pinned always show their name.
- The gear icon next to the flags opens the menu with "Places", "Data" and "Colors". "Places" lists all your pins; select one to jump to it.
- Use the flag buttons to switch between German and English.

## Your data and backups

- Pins are stored only in the browser or app on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- Under "Data" (gear menu), choose "Save as file" to download a backup (JSON file).
- To restore, choose "Data" > "Choose file", or paste the text.
- Never upload your backup file anywhere else. It contains your places and notes.
